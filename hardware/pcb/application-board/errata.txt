Diode D101 isn't required if the Picos aren't going to
have USB cables plugged in. The present physical build
is such that it's not possible to plug USB cables into
the Pico's connectors, which makes that diode redundant.



ET Q202 is oriented on the board the wrong way round.
Schematic is correct, but I have the footprint with the
drain and source the wrong way round.



J101 and J103 are the 4-way connectors for the OLED screens.
Because the screen have their connections at the top, those
PCB connectors really should be halfway down the PCB facing
the top. That removes the need to bend the 4-way ribbon
cable.



On the main board v1.1 design I've added a software link from
the support Pico to the right side Pico (Pico1). This can't be
a UART because the support Pico doesn't have a spare one. But
it could be a transputer link or similar. It's 2 pins on the
main board connector which need to go to 2 pins on Pico1.
